This low-molecular-weight form of hyaluronic acid serves as one of nature's most powerful humectants, capable of holding up to 1,000 times its weight in water. Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)
Vitamin C is a cornerstone of effective anti-ageing skincare, offering multiple benefits through its role as both an antioxidant and a cofactor in collagen synthesis. As an antioxidant, it neutralizes free radicals generated by UV exposure, pollution, and normal metabolic processes, thereby protecting cellular structures from oxidative damage. Its brightening properties help to even skin tone and reduce the appearance of age spots and hyperpigmentation. Perhaps most significantly, vitamin C is essential for the hydroxylation of proline and lysine amino acids, a critical step in the formation of stable collagen molecules that provide structural support to the skin.
Vitamin E (Tocopheryl Acetate)
Working in synergy with vitamin C, vitamin E provides complementary antioxidant protection whilst offering additional benefits for skin health. This fat-soluble antioxidant is particularly effective at protecting cell membranes from lipid peroxidation, a damaging process accelerated by environmental stressors. Tocopheryl acetate, the stable ester form used in the UltraLift Serum, converts to active vitamin E within the skin, where it helps neutralize free radicals, reduce inflammation, and support the skin's healing processes. The combination of vitamins C and E creates a more robust antioxidant defence system than either vitamin alone.
Glycerin
Often overlooked despite its importance, glycerin is a highly effective humectant and emollient that plays a crucial role in maintaining skin hydration and barrier integrity. It draws moisture from the environment and deeper skin layers to the surface, where it helps prevent transepidermal water loss. Glycerin also supports the skin's natural moisturizing factor (NMF), a complex mixture of compounds that keep the stratum corneum hydrated and functioning properly. Its gentle, non-irritating nature makes it suitable for all skin types, including sensitive skin.
